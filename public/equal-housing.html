FinRateCalc.com wholeheartedly supports and abides by the principles of the Fair Housing Act (Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968) and the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA).

1. Our Non-Discrimination Commitment

We are dedicated to fostering transparent, unbiased, and accessible financial tools for all prospective homebuyers, mortgage borrowers, and real estate investors across all 50 US States.

FinRateCalc does not discriminate against any individual or group based on:

  • Race, Color, or National Origin

  • Familial Status (having children under age 18, or seeking legal custody)

  • Age, Marital Status, or Receipt of Public Assistance (ECOA protections)

FinRateCalc.com does not underwrite loans, accept mortgage applications, originate credit, or make credit determinations. We urge all users to engage with HUD-approved housing counseling agencies and reputable, licensed equal housing opportunity lenders when seeking residential mortgage financing.
