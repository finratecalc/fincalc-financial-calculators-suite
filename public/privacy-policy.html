Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to FinRateCalc.com ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide precision financial calculation tools engineered for US homeowners, tax filers, borrowers, and investors. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Financial Privacy)

Your privacy is our priority. FinRateCalc does not store, transmit, or sell the financial numbers you enter into our calculation modules (including mortgage amounts, annual salaries, take-home wages, credit card balances, or 401(k) contributions). All mathematical operations are computed locally in your web browser or processed strictly during session runtime without persistent retention.

2. Data We May Automatically Collect

When you visit FinRateCalc.com, we or our third-party infrastructure providers may automatically record standard browser technical logs:

  • Device architecture, browser type, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/state level)
  • Referring URLs and timestamps of page interactions

3. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may partner with third-party advertising vendors (such as Google AdSense) and analytical services to maintain free access to our tools:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their navigation patterns.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to request what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the sale of personal information. FinRateCalc does not sell consumer personal information.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact channels at support@finratecalc.com.
